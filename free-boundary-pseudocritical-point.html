Pseudocritical point of the spread-out Ising model with free boundaries at the upper critical dimension in 1 ≤ d ≤ 4

Jiwoon Park
November 20, 2026

Manuscript PDF · LaTeX source on GitHub · Research repository

Abstract

We study the susceptibility of the spread-out Ising model on a cube of volume V = nd with free boundary conditions. The interaction (−ΔR)d/4 places the model at its upper critical dimension for each d ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4}. For sufficiently large fixed spread R, we prove that there is an inverse temperature at which the effective potential of the principal mode has zero curvature. At this pseudocritical point the susceptibility has order √(V log V). At the bulk critical point, Camia, Jiang and Newman obtain order n2 for nearest-neighbour Ising boxes with free boundaries in d ≥ 4 under a bulk two-point hypothesis [12]. The logarithmic factor at our pseudocritical point also occurs for the critical weakly coupled |φ|4 model on the four-dimensional torus [31] and in the free-boundary pseudocritical window of the four-dimensional hierarchical model [28].

Main theorem

Fix d ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4} and a sufficiently large spread radius R. On the cube Λ = {1, …, n}d, take the interaction (−ΔR)d/4 on the full lattice and delete the bonds to the exterior. Write χFΛ for the variance of the total spin divided by the volume V.

For every n ≥ n0(R), there is an inverse temperature τpcΛ at which the effective potential of the principal mode has zero curvature at the origin. At this point,

cd,R √(V log V) ≤ χFΛ(τpcΛ) ≤ Cd,R √(V log V),

with positive finite constants independent of n. In four dimensions the susceptibility therefore has order n2√(log n).
